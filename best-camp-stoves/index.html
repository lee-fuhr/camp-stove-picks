Quick answer

The Jetboil Flash is our top pick for most campers – it boils water rapidly in an integrated system that’s hard to beat.

For backpackers, the MSR PocketRocket 2 wins on weight savings; car campers will love the Coleman Classic two-burner; and budget hikers should consider the BRS Ultralight.

A good camp stove turns a bag of dehydrated mush into a hot meal after a long day on the trail. Whether you’re melting snow at altitude or flipping pancakes at a drive-up site, the right stove saves time, fuel, and frustration. The key is matching the stove to your trip: weight, fuel type, cookware, and how many mouths you’re feeding all matter. Start by thinking about where you’ll camp and what you’ll cook. Backpackers need something light and compact; car campers can haul a bigger unit with multiple burners. Fuel availability matters, too, canister stoves are convenient, while liquid-fuel models let you refill from bottles in remote areas. We’ve researched owner feedback and expert reviews to highlight five stoves that cover these choices well.

Best for specific needs

Best for backpacking

Weight and packability are everything on a long hike. The MSR PocketRocket 2 delivers an excellent power-to-weight ratio and lets you pair it with any pot you like. It simmers well for a canister stove and has stood the test of time in the backcountry. Our pick: MSR PocketRocket 2.

Best for car camping

When you have the space, a two-burner stove makes camp cooking much more enjoyable. The Coleman Classic is affordable, durable, and can handle everything from a big pot of chili to a frying pan full of bacon. It’s the go-to for families and groups who cook real meals at the site. Our pick: Coleman Classic Propane Gas Camping Stove.

Best for international travel

Different countries have different fuel standards, and a multi-fuel stove avoids the headache of hunting for specific canisters. The MSR WhisperLite International burns white gas, kerosene, and even unleaded auto fuel, and it’s built to last in remote environments. Our pick: MSR WhisperLite International Multi-Fuel Stove.

Our verdict

For the vast majority of campers – whether you are backpacking, car camping, or taking off on a round-the-world trip – the Jetboil Flash offers the best combination of speed, convenience, and thoughtful design. But if weight is your first priority, go with the MSR PocketRocket 2; if you’re cooking for a crowd, grab the Coleman two-burner; and if you’re bound for far-flung places, the WhisperLite International will never let you down. Pick the stove that fits the way you camp, and you’ll eat well every trip.
