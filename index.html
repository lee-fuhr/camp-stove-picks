How we choose our picks

We don’t test stoves ourselves. Instead, our recommendations come from sifting through thousands of owner reviews, long-term usage reports on hiking forums, and published expert tests (like those from OutdoorGearLab and SectionHiker). We track what actually breaks, leaky valves, finicky igniters, brittle pot supports, and only recommend stoves with a proven track record of reliability over several seasons. Feedback matters more than hype. We prioritize stoves that have been in production for years, with consistent positive feedback from backpackers, campers, and weekend chefs.
